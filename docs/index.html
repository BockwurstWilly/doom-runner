How to play

  1. Download the ZIP and extract it anywhere.
  2. Double-click "Doom Runner.bat" – done, the game starts with Freedoom.
  3. Recommended: original Doom II. Copy your DOOM2.WAD into the extracted folder (next to the .bat). It is in your Steam or GOG Doom II folder, on Steam e.g. steamapps\common\Doom 2\base\.
  4. Recommended: Brutal Doom. Get it from ModDB and put its .pk3 file (name starts with "brutal") into the same folder. It is loaded automatically.
Run & gunYour marine runs and shoots on his own – you steer left and right with the mouse.
Gates & squadShoot the gates to upgrade them, pick up marines and build your own squad.
Hordes & bossesZombie hordes, barrels, weapon crates – and tough bosses. Collect souls, upgrade, repeat.

Controls: mouse left/right = steer · click = buy upgrade / start · keyboard: A/D or arrow keys, 1/2/3, Space · ESC = menu. Progress is saved automatically. The game can be switched between English and German in the main menu.
